    Materials

    Your course files - summaries, practice questions, and the exams and deadlines from a syllabus.
    • Make one folder per course - questions and exams are matched to the course by it.
    • On each file: Summarize writes a summary, Questions makes practice questions for Study, and Exams & deadlines reads a syllabus - exams go to the Planner, submissions to Tasks.
